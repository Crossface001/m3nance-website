M3NANCE

M3NANCE is a fast-rising underground artist building a name for himself and a strong persona among upcoming artists. He is the creator of the unique sound called M3-NANCE, or as he likes to call it, the sound of chaos. He is entering the mainstream industry with a song he personally considers not good, but his fans say otherwise. His hit single, GUCCI GANG, features Afrocyclops, also known as Voleskey, on November 6, 2025, a little before the tragic passing of his mother.

M3NANCE, whose real name is Kokoete Daniel Akpan, hails from Edo State, Benin City, Nigeria. Despite being from Akwa Ibom, he was born on November 11, 2008, at the same time the presidential election was taking place, when Adams Ali Oshomole won and became president of Nigeria. Born to humble beginnings with his single mother and no siblings, he began crafting his sound at the young age of 9 and was already known locally to his family and peers as a rapper. He later transitioned into singing sometime in 2022 and has switched between creating music and producing beats.
